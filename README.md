# PiP Mate for Safari

## Your video, one keystroke away.

On Mac, press **P** to toggle Picture-in-Picture and **O** to open or close playback controls. Keep a tutorial beside your work, change speed in seconds, capture a useful frame, and step backward to review a detail.

Safari video tools for **Mac, iPhone, and iPad**. This repository contains product information, support, and privacy documentation.

## Features

- **Native Picture-in-Picture:** activate from the Safari extension button on supported videos.
- **Quick playback speed:** choose a preset or a custom speed from 0.1× to 4×.
- **Snapshots:** save the current video frame to your device where supported.
- **Reverse stepping:** revisit details in seekable videos. This is stepped review; frame accuracy varies by player.
- **Mac keyboard controls:** P for PiP, O for settings. Preset speed changes apply immediately and close the panel. Drag the settings title bar to reposition it.
- **iPhone and iPad controls:** use the compact floating tools for playback speed and other video actions. Keyboard shortcuts are not required.

### Set up on Mac

1. Open Safari > Settings > Extensions.
2. Enable PiP Mate for Safari and allow access to the sites you want to use.
3. Play a video and click it to select it. Click the extension icon or press P to toggle PiP.
4. Press O for settings. Shortcuts work when you are not typing in a text field.

### Set up on iPhone or iPad

1. Enable PiP Mate in Settings > Apps > Safari > Extensions. On older systems, Safari is directly in Settings.
2. Allow website access, then open and play a video in Safari.
3. Open Safari’s page menu and choose PiP Mate to activate PiP on supported videos.
4. Use the floating controls to change speed. If hidden, open PiP Mate’s extension settings and enable the floating tools again.

### Troubleshooting

- **No video detected:** start playback, check site access, refresh the page, and try again.
- **PiP unavailable:** availability depends on Safari and the site’s player. Try the system fullscreen player and its PiP control if available. Simulator results can differ from a physical device.
- **P or O does not respond:** click outside text fields, select the video, and check extension permissions.
- **Snapshot fails:** protected or cross-origin video may prevent frame capture.
- **Reverse stepping unavailable:** live streams and non-seekable videos cannot be stepped backward.
- **Captions:** enable the site’s captions first. Support depends on caption source and Safari; macOS may apply its own caption styling. No transcription or translation is provided, and captions embedded in the image cannot be resized.
- **Automatic next-video playback:** handover depends on the site starting the next video and Safari’s background behavior. It is not guaranteed on every site or when the browser is minimized.

## Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

### Data handled by the app

PiP Mate for Safari does not collect or upload personal information, browsing history, webpage content, or video content to the developer. It includes no advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

### Website access

The extension uses the website access you grant to identify videos and provide Picture-in-Picture, playback speed controls, snapshots, reverse stepping, and supported caption features. Processing takes place on your device.

### Preferences and snapshots

Preferences retained by the extension are stored on your device. Snapshots are generated only when you request them and saved through the browser or system. PiP Mate does not upload your snapshots.

### Third-party services

Websites you visit, Apple’s App Store and TestFlight, and this GitHub support site operate under their own privacy policies. Information you voluntarily submit in a support request is handled by GitHub and may be public. Do not include sensitive information in issues.

### Privacy questions

Contact the developer through the [support issues page](https://github.com/hyvasslli/pip-mate-for-safari/issues). Changes to this policy will be published here with an updated date.
